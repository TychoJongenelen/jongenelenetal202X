Monte Carlo Simulation Output
=============================

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Variable Name         | Unit                  | Description
----------------------------------------------------------------------

# NH₃ FLUXES
flux_tot             | ng m⁻² s⁻¹            | Total NH₃ flux (net exchange)
flux_stom            | ng m⁻² s⁻¹            | NH₃ flux to/from stomata
flux_w               | ng m⁻² s⁻¹            | NH₃ flux to/from wet surfaces
flux_soil            | ng m⁻² s⁻¹            | NH₃ flux to/from soil surface

# CUMULATIVE FLUXES
cumflux_obs          | kg NH3 ha⁻¹           | Observed cumulative NH₃ flux
cumflux_mod          | kg NH3 ha⁻¹           | Modeled cumulative NH₃ flux

# TIME VARIABLE
datetime             | ms since 1970-01-01   | Timestamp in Unix epoch time (milliseconds)

----------------------------------------------------------------------

File Naming Convention:
- The Monte Carlo output files follow the format:  
  `{model}_mc_{percentile}`  
  Example: `DEPAC_mc_025`, `DEPAC_mc_975`
  
- `{model}` represents the model name, such as `DEPAC`, `massad`, or `zhang`.
- `{percentile}` indicates the percentile of the distribution:
  - **025** represents the 2.5th percentile (lower bound of the confidence interval).
  - **975** represents the 97.5th percentile (upper bound of the confidence interval).

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes:
- NH₃ fluxes are expressed in **nanograms per square meter per second (ng m⁻² s⁻¹)**.
- Cumulative NH₃ fluxes are given in **kilograms per hectare (kg NH₃ ha⁻¹)**.
- The **datetime** field represents a Unix timestamp in **milliseconds** since January 1, 1970 (UTC).
  This format is processed within the scripts located in `/figure_scripts/`.
- The sum of the compartment-specific fluxes (`flux_stom`, `flux_w`, `flux_soil`) does not necessarily match 
  `flux_tot`. This discrepancy arises because the datasets represent percentile-based values rather than 
  direct summations.

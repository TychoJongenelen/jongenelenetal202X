The statistics in p_table2_SA_statistics_formatted.csv represent the percentage change 
in the average NH₃ flux resulting from perturbing or modifying a variable to its lower 
or upper value, as listed in Table A1 of "Validation and Uncertainty Quantification of 
Three State-of-the-Art Ammonia Surface Exchange Schemes Using NH₃ Flux Measurements in a 
Dune Ecosystem" (Jongenelen et al., 2025). Positive values indicate an increase in 
deposition, while negative values indicate a decrease.
